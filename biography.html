The beginning

SEVEN YEARS OLD.
ONE BIG JUMP.

Born on 13 July 2007 in Esplugues de Llobregat and raised around Mataró, Yamal joined FC Barcelona at seven from CF La Torreta. Barça's own profile describes a player who moved through the academy age groups unusually quickly, standing out for daring dribbling, chance creation and the ability to play across the attack.

Career timeline

RECORDS IN
FAST FORWARD.

29 APRIL 2023

BARÇA FIRST-TEAM DEBUT

He debuted against Real Betis aged 15 years, 9 months and 16 days, becoming Barcelona's youngest first-team player.

2023–24

FROM PROSPECT TO REGULAR

Yamal became a regular first-team presence and began stacking club and international age records while still eligible for youth football.

SUMMER 2024

EUROPEAN CHAMPION

With Spain, he became the youngest player to appear at a European Championship and then the youngest player to feature in a major international final as Spain won Euro 2024.

27 MAY 2025

BARÇA CONTRACT TO 2031

FC Barcelona announced a contract extension tying Yamal to the club through 30 June 2031.

2025/26

LALIGA PLAYER OF THE SEASON

Barça reported 16 league goals and 11 assists as Yamal helped the club retain the LaLiga title and was named the competition's player of the season.

2026

WORLD CHAMPION WITH SPAIN

FC Barcelona's current official player profile lists the 2026 World Cup among Yamal's honours with Spain.
