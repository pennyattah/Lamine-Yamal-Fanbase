LY10 / Privacy

YOUR
PRIVACY.

A short, plain-English explanation of what this independent fan project stores and sends.

Browser preferences

Theme, accessibility settings, predictions and demo poll choices may be stored locally in your browser using localStorage. LY10 does not have a visitor account system.

Feedback and matchday signup forms

If you choose to submit a feedback message or email address, the form is sent to the LY10 project inbox using FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

Site analytics

LY10 may use Vercel Web Analytics for aggregate site-performance and visitor metrics when enabled for the hosting project. LY10 does not use Microsoft Clarity session recording.

External links

Links to FC Barcelona, LaLiga, social platforms and editorial sources are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

Simple rule

Collect less.

LY10 is built as a static fan project. It does not need passwords, profiles or unnecessary personal data to provide match information and stories.
