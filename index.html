04 / BEYOND THE TOUCHLINE

THE PERSON
BEHIND THE 10.

A fan dossier about the roots, people and values that travel with him.

ROOTS

ROCAfONDA
RAISED.

Born in Esplugues de Llobregat and raised in Rocafonda, Mataró, Lamine’s story began in a working-class home with a ball, a park and a big dream.

SPANISHMOROCCAN HERITAGEEQUATOGUINEAN HERITAGE
FAMILY

HIS FIRST
TEAM.

His mother, Sheila Ebana, is from Equatorial Guinea and his father, Mounir Nasraoui, has Moroccan heritage. He has spoken warmly about remaining close to both parents and to younger siblings Keyne and Baraa.

Publicly shared family background
THE NAME

LAMINE
YAMAL.

“Lamine Yamal” is his given name; Nasraoui Ebana are his surnames. Reports say the given names honour people who helped his family before he was born.

NASRAOUI · EBANA
01

OFF THE PITCH

Friends and teammates describe a relaxed, funny, family-minded competitor. In his downtime, he is known to enjoy music, video games and being around the squad — while keeping much of his personal life private.

02

THE CIRCLE

His Barcelona and Spain generation includes Pau Cubarsí, Pedri, Gavi, Alejandro Balde and Nico Williams. Their shared confidence is part of the new-era energy around club and country.

03

PUBLIC LIFE

Coverage has linked him with Alex Padilla and, later, Inés García. This fan page treats relationship reporting carefully: only publicly shared appearances belong here; social-media rumor does not.

2007 / A FULL-CIRCLE MOMENT

MESSI &
THE DREAM.

Lamine’s role model, Lionel Messi, helped his mother give five-month-old Lamine a bath during a UNICEF charity-calendar shoot. Neither could have known the story they had begun.

READ THE FULL UNICEF STORY ↗

In 2026, Yamal became a UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador, supporting every child’s right to play. It is a natural continuation of the values he connects to his own childhood: family, a ball, a park and possibility.
