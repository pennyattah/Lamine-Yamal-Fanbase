BARÇA 5–2 RAYO:
YAMAL HITS 50 AND THEN 51.

BARÇA5 — 2RAYO

Barcelona came from behind to make it three La Liga wins from three, with Raphinha and Lamine Yamal both scoring twice and a Florian Lejeune own goal completing the five.

LY10 Yamal focus: Lamine Yamal scored twice. His first, a left-footed strike into the top corner, was his 50th goal for Barcelona's first team; his second arrived in the 90th minute with a measured finish inside the post.

What happened

Rayo struck first through Sergio Camello, but Barcelona responded quickly. Raphinha equalised in the 19th minute before Yamal put Barça ahead with a spectacular effort from the edge of the area. After half-time, Lejeune turned Anthony Gordon's low ball into his own net.

Camello pulled one back for Rayo, but Raphinha restored the two-goal cushion. Yamal then had the final word in the 90th minute, completing his brace and sealing a 5–2 victory.

Why the Yamal performance mattered

The brace moved Yamal to 51 first-team goals for Barcelona and gave him his first two league goals of the 2026/27 season. The official club report also noted that he was lively from the start and consistently dangerous in the final third.
