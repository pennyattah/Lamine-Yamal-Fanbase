BARÇA 2–0 ATHLETIC:
YAMAL KEEPS THE RIGHT SIDE ELECTRIC.

BARÇA2 — 0ATHLETIC

Raphinha opened the scoring and Fermín López made the result safe late on as Barcelona won their first home league game of the 2026/27 season.

LY10 Yamal focus: no goal or assist is credited here. The official Barça report instead highlights repeated shooting danger, a second-half effort off the underside of the bar, skill that won a dangerous free kick, and a pass that released Anthony Gordon.

What happened

Barcelona controlled long stretches of the game but Athletic goalkeeper Unai Simón repeatedly kept the score down. Raphinha finally broke through in the 37th minute after a Pedri pass through the lines, taking Barça into half-time 1–0 ahead.

Yamal had already forced saves before the break and came especially close after half-time when a shot struck the underside of the crossbar. Athletic still threatened through Oihan Sancet, who hit the post, before Fermín scored in the 82nd minute after a late attacking move involving substitutes Jules Koundé and Karim Adeyemi.

Why the Yamal performance mattered

The value of the performance was pressure rather than a headline goal contribution. His repeated attempts stretched Athletic's defensive shape, and his ability to create danger from the right kept Barça's attack active even when Simón was winning individual battles.
