ELCHE 0–5 BARÇA:
A FIVE-GOAL STATEMENT.

Barcelona opened their league campaign with a 5–0 away win at Martínez Valero, powered by two goals each from Raphinha and Fermín López plus a debut goal for Karim Adeyemi.
